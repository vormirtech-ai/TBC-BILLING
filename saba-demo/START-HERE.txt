SABA — FINE DINING BILLING & KOT
Demonstration build


TO OPEN IT
──────────
Double-click   Saba-Demo.html

That is the whole system in one file. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or
Safari. There is nothing to install, no server to start, and no internet
connection required — it works with the network cable pulled out.

installation issues one PIN per person and never displays it.


THE SIX-MINUTE TOUR
───────────────────
The system opens in the middle of a service, so there is something real on
every screen from the first second. Inside the app, the "Guide" button at the
bottom of the navigation rail walks through the same six moments and will take
you to each screen.

  1. FLOOR      Eight tables in service. The stripe down each card says what is
                happening. Table 6 has a red dot — a plate has been sitting on
                the pass too long.

  2. ORDER      Open terrace table G2. Pick a seat number, tap a dish. Try the
                Barg Fillet: it asks for doneness. Nothing has gone to the
                kitchen yet.

  3. FIRE       Press Fire. One order becomes one docket per kitchen section —
                the tandoor never sees the dessert line.

  4. KITCHEN    The pass, read at three metres. Colour means one thing: how long
                this has waited against that station's target. Bump a docket and
                watch the floor plan change behind you.

  5. BILLS      Open table 6, press Split, choose "by seat". Each share carries
                its own share of the service charge and tax, and the three add
                back to exactly the one bill.

  6. REPORTS    Thirty days of trading, kitchen timings by station, and an audit
                tab listing every void, comp and discount with the reason and
                the manager who approved it.


IF THE DEMO GOES OFF THE RAILS
──────────────────────────────
Setup → "Start the evening again" puts every table, docket and bill back to
exactly how it opened. Nothing else on the computer is touched.


FOR YOUR DEVELOPERS
───────────────────
README.md has the full feature list, the architecture and the honest list of
what a live installation would still need to add.

The readable source is in src/ — plain ES modules, no framework, no
dependencies. `node tests/all.mjs` runs 72 tests; `node build.mjs` regenerates
Saba-Demo.html from the source.
