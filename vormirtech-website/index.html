How we work

Four steps, no surprises

You always know what is being built, what it costs and when it lands.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We sit with the people who will use the thing — the cashier, the site engineer, the sales desk — and map how the work actually happens today.

  2. 02

    Design

    Screens, flows and a fixed scope you sign off before a line of code is written. No moving targets, no surprise invoices.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short cycles with something you can click at the end of each one. You see progress weekly, not at the end.

  4. 04

    Launch & train

    We install it, train your staff on it and stay reachable afterwards. Then we market it, if that is part of the brief.

About us

A Nagpur studio for businesses that need both halves

Vormir Tech Solutions started with a simple observation: a business rarely needs only software or only marketing. It needs a system that runs the operation and a way to bring customers to it — and it needs one team accountable for both.

So that is what we built. Seven-plus businesses now run on work we designed, wrote, deployed and trained their staff on. When something breaks at nine at night, you call the people who built it.

  • Offline-first where it matters. A billing counter cannot stop because the connection did.

  • One team, one number to call. Design, build, deploy, train and market — under one roof.
